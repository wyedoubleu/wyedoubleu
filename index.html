I am a second-year Ph.D. student at Curtin University, supervised by Prof. Garenth Lim and Jonathan Phang. I work on 3D virtual try-on. My goal is to help machines understand what a person is wearing in a 3D scan, separate the garments from the body, and drape new clothing onto a digital human.

My research sits at the intersection of computer vision, point cloud processing and deep learning. Before my Ph.D., I completed a bachelor's degree in Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Curtin University and interned at Intel and AI3D PLT.

Publications

  • Recovering the Textured Underlying Body from Clothed 3D Scans

    GECOST 2026 · Accepted

    A two-stage method that first predicts a sparse inner body beneath the clothing, then reconstructs the full textured underlying body from the clothed scan.

    Yi Wei Fung, King Hann Lim, Jonathan Then Sien Phang

    • Paper

  • PartCloth3D: Point Cloud Clothing Segmentation with Body Part Priors

    Image and Vision Computing · under review

    A clothing segmentation framework for 3D body scans. A body part segmentation module refined by graph-cut optimisation supplies body priors, which are fused with point and clothing-class features to label every point.

    Yi Wei Fung, King Hann Lim, Jonathan Then Sien Phang

    • Paper
    • Code
    • Project

  • Review on Three-Dimensional Clothing Modelling and Digital Human Reconstruction for Virtual Try-On Applications

    Multimedia Tools and Applications, 2026 · Open access

    A review of the full 3D virtual try-on pipeline, covering garment creation, human avatar reconstruction and fitting garments onto avatars, with a comparison of the methods and datasets used at each stage.

    Yi Wei Fung, King Hann Lim, Jonathan Then Sien Phang
