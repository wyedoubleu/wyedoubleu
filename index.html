I am a PhD student at Curtin University , supervised by Prof. Garenth Lim King Hann and Jonathan Phang. My research focuses on developing an interactive 3D try-on system that connects users' personalized avatars with online fashion platforms. I'm driven by one question: how do we make digital fashion fit as well as real fashion does?

I completed a bachelor degree in Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Curtin University in 2023. Previously, I was an engineering intern at Intel working on CPU structural design and silicon validation. I also joined AI3D PLT as a research intern to work on 3D human reconstruction for 2D virtual try-on.

I am seeking research internships and collaborations in human-centric AI and related fields. Feel free to reach out if you are working on a problem and my background could help. 🌱

Selected Publications

  • TextureInner: Recovering the Textured Underlying Body from Clothed 3D Scans

    GECOST 2027 · Accepted

    We recover the textured body beneath clothing from a 3D scan by predicting clothing-to-body displacement using SE(3)-equivariant tightness features and inpainting occluded regions via vertex recoloring.

    Yi Wei Fung, King Hann Lim, Jonathan Then Sien Phang

  • PartCloth3D: Point Cloud Clothing Segmentation with Body Part Priors

    Image and Vision Computing · Under Review

    A clothing segmentation framework design for raw and noisy 3D human scans using predicted body parts as a prior and uses them to correlate the relationship between human points and clothing categories. It can segment background points that SoTA methods 4D-Dress and CloSe-Net do not handle.

    Yi Wei Fung, King Hann Lim, Jonathan Then Sien Phang

  • Review on Three-Dimensional Clothing Modelling and Digital Human Reconstruction for Virtual Try-On Applications

    Multimedia Tools and Applications, 2026 · Open access

    This paper presents a comprehensive review of 3D virtual try-on, covering three core components: 3D garment generation, 3D human avatar reconstruction, and body-garment transformation. It provides an up-to-date research progress, examines the strengths and limitations of current 3D VTO methods, explores practical applications, identifies existing challenges, and suggests promising directions for future research.

    Yi Wei Fung, King Hann Lim, Jonathan Then Sien Phang
