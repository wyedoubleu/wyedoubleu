## Hello World 👋

I'm Yi Wei, you can call me Evie. 😄 a girl figuring life out. 

Please find my personal webpage at https://wyedoubleu.github.io/. 

My research focus on developing an interactive and personalized virtual try-on system. My goals is to enhance online shopping experience by letting consumers to see, explore, and interact with clothing items on their personalize avatar. 

### 🔬 Research Experience
I have worked on:
- 3D human body reconstruction and registration from 3D scans
- Clothing segmentation on point clouds and garment decomposition
- Body and clothing measurement from 3D scans
- Recovering the underlying body from clothed 3D scans
- Clothing transfer and 3D garment draping on digital humans
- 2D virtual try-on and depth estimation
  
### 📚 Currently Learning
I'm taking courses on **LLM**, **agentic AI** and **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models** to explore how they can make virtual try-on systems more interactive and intelligent.

### 🤝 Let's Collaborate
I'm open to research internship opportunities or research collaborations. Feel free to reach out about potential projects or just to chat!
